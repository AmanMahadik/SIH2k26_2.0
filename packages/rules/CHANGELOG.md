# Regulatory Rules Changelog

All rule modifications in this package are strictly traceable to published AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) clauses and Gazette Notifications.

## [v1.0.0] - 2026-04-01 (AY 2026-27 Baseline)
### Added
- **`R-DOC-AFFIDAVIT`**: Mandatory affidavit submission under APH 2024-27 §4.1.2.
- **`R-DOC-NOC`**: Mandatory State DTE / Affiliating University NOC under APH 2024-27 §4.1.4.
- **`R-DOC-LAND`**: Barrier-free infrastructure and land compliance under APH 2024-27 §4.2.1.
- **`R-EXP-NOC`**: NOC validity check across AY 2026-27 under APH 2024-27 §4.3.1.
- **`R-EXP-AFFIDAVIT`**: 180-day affidavit execution recency window under APH 2024-27 §4.1.3.
- **`R-XDOC-NAME`**: 90% Levenshtein-tolerant cross-document legal name reconciliation under APH 2024-27 §4.4.2.
- **`R-REC-INTAKE`**: Intake threshold reconciliation against AICTE Central Master under APH 2024-27 §4.5.1.
- **`R-TER-FEE`**: Technical Education Regulatory fee payment validation under APH 2024-27 §4.6.1.
